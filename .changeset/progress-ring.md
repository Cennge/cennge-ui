`ProgressRing`: progress towards a goal as a ring, or one ring per goal nested. Past the goal a ring goes round again over its start, with a shadow where it overlaps. Each ring is a meter for screen readers, its words in `labels`. `ChartColor` is now exported from `cennge-ui` too, and the provider's `reducedMotion="always"` cuts animation delays as well as durations.
