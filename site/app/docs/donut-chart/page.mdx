## When to use

Use a DonutChart to show how one whole splits into a few parts, six at most: minutes by repository, runs by result. The ring gives the proportions at a glance and the legend beside it gives each part's value and share, which is where the eye reads them.

## Similar

- [BarChart](/docs/bar-chart/) compares the same values by length, one bar each: the better choice when they are many or close.
- [AreaChart](/docs/area-chart/) with `isStacked` shows how a whole splits over time, not at one moment.
- [Progress](/docs/progress/) shows one part of one whole, such as a task 70% done.

## A ring with its total

`index` names each part and `value` holds its amount. The middle shows the total over `labels.total`; a hovered, tapped or focused part takes its place. The numbers on this page are sample data.

## A pie

`variant="pie"` fills the middle, so there is no total in it: the picked part's legend row lights up and the other parts fade. Here theme colours carry a meaning: passed in `success`, failed in `danger`, cancelled in the grey of `default`.

## The small parts as Other

`colors` gives the parts their colours in order; without it they take the palette's slots. `default` is the grey for a part with no colour of its own, such as the rest folded into Other. `valueFormat` writes the amounts in the middle, the legend and the table; the shares stay percentages in the provider's locale.

## Nothing to divide

A part of zero or less is left out of the ring, and the arrow keys skip it, though the legend still lists it. When no part is left, `emptyContent` sits in an empty grey ring, or `labels.empty` ("No data") without it, the legend is gone and nothing takes focus. The table screen readers get still lists every part, at zero.

## Accessibility

- The figure and the ring are named by `aria-label`; the ring is a focusable group described by `labels.keyboardHint`.
- Focus from the keyboard picks the first part at once, and a polite live region reads each part the arrows reach, as "enhancement: 8 (47.1%)".
- The picked part's legend row lights up; the rows of the others keep their full contrast.
- Screen readers get a table of every part with its value and share, and a last row with the total.

## Server rendering

Nothing in a DonutChart waits for a measure: the ring, the total, the legend and the table are all rendered on the server. The ring pops in as it appears; with reduced motion it is there at once.
