## When to use

Use a TimeInput for a time of day: when a call starts, when a nightly job runs, when a release goes out. The field shows the time the way the locale writes it, with AM and PM or without, and the value is always `"HH:mm"` on a 24-hour clock, like `<input type="time">`.

Typing is forgiving: `14:30`, `14.30`, `1430`, `930`, `9`, `2:30 pm`, `2p`. The text is read when the field loses focus or on <kbd>Enter</kbd>; without AM or PM the hour counts on a 24-hour clock.

## Similar

- [DatePicker](/docs/date-picker/) takes the date; with a TimeInput next to it you have a date and a time, as in the example below.
- [NumberInput](/docs/number-input/) is for an amount of time, such as a timeout in minutes, rather than a time of day.

## Type it or step it

Arrow keys work on the part under the caret: the hours move by one, the minutes by `step`, and on a 12-hour clock the AM or PM switches the half of the day. They stop at `minValue` and `maxValue`. A typed time is kept to the minute, not rounded to the step. In an empty field they count from `minValue`, or from the current time. Emptying the field sets the value to `null`.

## 12 or 24 hours

The clock comes from `Intl` for the `locale`: 12 hours in the United States, 24 in Britain. `hourCycle` overrides it. These three share one value: change it in any of them.

## A date and a time

Name both fields and the form sends `"2026-11-19"` and `"10:00"`; join them as `` `${date}T${time}` ``. The result is a local date and time with no time zone, which is what a person picks; turn it into an instant where you know whose time zone it is.

## In another language

`formatError` receives `invalid` with the pattern, or `outOfRange` with the limits as `"HH:mm"`. The clock, the AM and PM words it reads and shows, and the format follow the `locale` of [CenngeProvider](/docs/provider/) or of the field.

## Accessibility

The field is a text input with its label. The arrow keys change its text without typing, so a polite live region reads the new time after each step. A refusal sets `aria-invalid` and describes the field with the reason. On a 24-hour clock the field asks phones for the number pad; with AM and PM it keeps the letters.
