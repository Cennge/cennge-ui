## When to use

A Card groups what belongs to one thing, a repository, a release, a plan, and sets it apart from what is around it. When the whole card leads somewhere, make the card the link instead of putting a small link inside it.

## Similar

- [HoverCard](/docs/hover-card/) floats over a link while the pointer rests on it; a Card stays in the page.
- [Alert](/docs/alert/) is a message about the page, tinted by its colour; a Card holds content.
- [Accordion](/docs/accordion/) stacks sections that open and close; cards are always open.

## Header, body and footer

`CardHeader` lines up its children in a row, `CardBody` stacks them and grows to fill the card, `CardFooter` is a row of actions. All three are padded `div`s that take any class.

## Variants

`raised` lifts the card with a shadow. `bordered` swaps the shadow for an edge, for dense grids and lists. `flat` has neither and relies on the surface alone.

</Example>

## A whole card as a link

`asChild` renders the card as its child element, here a Next.js `Link`, and `isPressable` adds the hover, press and focus styles. It stays a real `<a>`: the browser shows its address, and it can be opened in a new tab.
