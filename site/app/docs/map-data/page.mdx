## When to use

Use MapGeoJSON to draw data you have as GeoJSON: areas, lines and points in one colour, or shaded by a number in their properties, a choropleth. Pair a choropleth with a MapLegend in the same colour and domain, so the shades can be read as numbers.

Use MapClusterLayer for many points that people pick one of: stores, pickup points, sensors. It groups the points that crowd, and a group zooms in until it comes apart.

## Similar

- [MapMarker](/docs/map-markers/) is a place with a popup, for a handful of them; a MapClusterLayer makes markers of thousands.
- [MapRoute](/docs/map-routes/) draws one line you build from points; MapGeoJSON draws whatever a GeoJSON file holds.
- A [BarChart](/docs/bar-chart/) of the same numbers by area name is easier to compare exactly; the map shows where they are.

## Shade areas by a number

`valueProperty` names the number in each feature's properties; `domain` sets the values at the two ends of the scale, the data's lowest and highest when you leave it out. Give the legend the same `domain`. A click reports the feature's properties to `onFeatureClick`.

## Areas, lines and points

Without `valueProperty` everything is drawn in one colour, each feature as what it is: areas filled and edged, lines stroked, points as dots. `data` can also be the URL of a GeoJSON file; then give `domain` too, as the lowest and highest values are read only from data passed as an object.

</Example>

## Thousands of points

MapClusterLayer takes your items and two functions: where each one is and what it is called. MapLibre builds the groups; the library draws them as markers sized by their count, and only the ones in view are in the page. A group's name comes from `clusterLabel`, so a reader can say the number they see to voice control.

</Example>

## Accessibility

- What MapGeoJSON draws is a picture on the canvas: screen readers do not read it, and its tooltip shows for a pointer or a tap, not for the keyboard. Give the same numbers another way as well, say a [Table](/docs/table/) of the areas beside the map.
- MapClusterLayer's groups are buttons in the tab order, named by `clusterLabel`. Its points are buttons too when you pass `onItemClick`, named by `getLabel`, which is also their tooltip.
- MapLegend is a group named by its `title` when the title is a string. Its numbers follow the provider's `locale` unless you pass `format`.
