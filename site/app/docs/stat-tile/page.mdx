## When to use

Use StatTile for the few numbers a dashboard opens with: steps or calories today, this week's commits, a weight, a latency. Give it the change since the period before, and say which way is good: a step count should go up, a body weight or a resting heart rate often down. A row of tiles reads as a summary; put a chart under a number when its history matters.

## Similar

- [Card](/docs/card/) is the surface StatTile is built like. Compose your own on a Card when a tile needs more than a number, a change and a chart.
- [Badge](/docs/badge/) is a short status on its own; the change in a StatTile is a badge tinted by whether it is good news.
- [Sparkline](/docs/sparkline/) is the trend that often goes under the number, in `chart`.

## A number and its change

`value` is written in the provider's locale when it is a number; pass a string to write it your way. `delta` is signed, and written with its sign and the `unit`.

## Which way is good

`goodDirection` is `"up"` by default. Set `"down"` where less is better and `"none"` for a number that is neither, such as deploys: its change stays grey.

## An icon and your own format

`icon` sits before the label. `formatDelta` writes the change, here as a percentage. `variant` is the [Card](/docs/card/)'s: `flat` by default, `bordered` or `raised`, and `radius` follows the provider's as a Card's does. In a narrow tile, two to a row on a phone, the value steps down a size.

## Accessibility

A StatTile is text, read in order: the label, the value with its unit, then the change and what it is measured against. The change is never colour alone: its sign is in the text, and screen readers hear "better" or "worse" after it, words you can change with `labels`. A change too small to show, such as 0.004 kg, is written as 0 and stays grey. A chart in `chart` brings its own name, such as a Sparkline's `aria-label`.
