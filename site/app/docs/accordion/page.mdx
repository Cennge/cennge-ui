## When to use

An Accordion shortens a long page of sections that are read one at a time: questions and answers, groups of settings, details under a summary. Each heading stays in view, so the reader sees what is there before opening it. When every section matters to everyone, leave them open as plain headings and text.

## Similar

- [Tabs](/docs/tabs/) show one panel in place of another, from a row of names; accordion sections open in the flow of the page and can be open together.
- [Card](/docs/card/) sets content apart but never hides it.

## Several open at once

With `selectionMode="multiple"` opening one section leaves the others as they are, and `value` is an array. `variant="splitted"` turns each item into a card of its own; `startContent` puts an icon before the title.

## Controlled, always one open

In single mode `value` is a string. `isCollapsible={false}` stops the open section from closing, so there is always one; its button then carries `aria-disabled`, so screen readers know it will not close. A disabled item keeps its place and title, but neither a click nor the keyboard opens it.
