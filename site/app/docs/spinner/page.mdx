## When to use

A Spinner marks something that is at work right now and has no amount to show: a job running, a panel fetching, a deploy on its way. It is sized to sit in a line of text or a list row. When you can say how much is done, a progress bar says more.

## Similar

- [Progress](/docs/progress/) shows how far a task has got; without a value its ring spins too, but it is a progressbar and comes in larger sizes.
- [Skeleton](/docs/skeleton/) holds the shape of content that is loading, so the page does not move when it arrives.
- [Button](/docs/button/) has its own spinner: pass `isLoading` instead of putting a Spinner inside.

## Sizes and colours

By default the ring is `currentColor`, so it matches the text it sits in. `color` picks a role colour instead; `default` is the muted grey.

## With a label, or without

Without `label` the spinner is hidden from screen readers, which suits a spinner next to words that already say what is going on. When it stands alone, `label` makes it a `role="status"` element named by that text.
