## When to use

Use BottomBar on a screen built around one action that people repeat, often one-handed: Log set, Add to breakfast, Save, Check out. Put the button the full width of the bar or at its end, and a short status beside it if it helps, such as "Set 3 of 4". One bar per screen.

## Similar

- [FloatingActionButton](/docs/floating-action-button/) floats one icon over the content; BottomBar gives an action a whole row and room for its label and a status.
- [BottomNavigation](/docs/bottom-navigation/) moves between screens; BottomBar acts on the one you are in.
- [Drawer](/docs/drawer/)'s `DrawerFooter` keeps buttons at the bottom of a sheet or panel, not of the screen.

## Fixed, sticky or static

`position="fixed"`, the default, pins it to the bottom of the screen. Over a fixed [BottomNavigation](/docs/bottom-navigation/) it stands on the navigation instead of under it, and it writes its own height to `--cennge-bottom-bar`, so a [FloatingActionButton](/docs/floating-action-button/) floats above both. Keep your page's end clear of it:

```tsx
<main className="pb-[calc(var(--cennge-bottom-bar,0px)+var(--cennge-bottom-nav,0px))]">…</main>
<BottomBar>
  <Button color="primary" fullWidth onClick={logSet}>Log set</Button>
</BottomBar>
```

`sticky` keeps it at the bottom of its scroll container, as on the plate; `static` leaves it in the flow.
