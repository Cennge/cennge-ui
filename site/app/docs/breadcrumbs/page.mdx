## When to use

Breadcrumbs show where a page sits in a hierarchy, a file in a repository or a page in a documentation site, and make each level above it one click away. They sit at the top of the page, under the main navigation, and they do not replace it: they only lead upwards.

## Similar

- [Pagination](/docs/pagination/) moves along a sequence of pages at the same level; Breadcrumbs move up the hierarchy.
- [Stepper](/docs/stepper/) shows how far a task has gone and what is left; Breadcrumbs show where the reader is, not what comes next.

## Collapse a long trail

With `maxItems`, a longer trail shows only that many items: `itemsBeforeCollapse` from the start (one unless you say otherwise), the rest from the end, and a … button between them. A click on it shows the whole trail.

## Next.js links

With `asChild` a `BreadcrumbItem` gives its look to your own link, here Next.js `Link`. `startContent` is not drawn in that case: put an icon inside the link, as the home icon here.

## Separators and sizes

`separator` takes any node, a slash here, in place of the chevron. `size` sets the text: `sm`, `md` or `lg`.

## Accessibility

The trail is a `<nav>` named "Breadcrumb", and the items are an ordered list, so screen readers say how many levels there are. Rename it with `aria-label` when a page has more than one trail or speaks another language. The last item becomes the current page unless you set its `isCurrent` yourself; `isCurrent={false}` keeps it a link. The … button is named "Show all breadcrumbs".
