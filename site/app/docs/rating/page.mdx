## When to use

Use a Rating to ask for a score on a short scale, five stars by default, and to show a score next to something: a release, a repository, a review. The same component reads both ways: interactive while people rate, `isReadOnly` when it shows an average such as 4.3.

## Similar

- [Slider](/docs/slider/) picks a number on a longer or finer scale.
- [Radio](/docs/radio/) names each choice in words; a Rating's choices are a count of stars.

## Rate something

The value is the number of stars, and `0` means no rating yet. A click on the chosen star, <kbd>Backspace</kbd> or <kbd>Delete</kbd> sets it back to `0`; pass `isClearable={false}` to keep a rating once given.

## Half stars

With `allowHalf`, the left half of a star picks 3.5 and the right half 4. The control becomes a single slider: the arrows step by half a star, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> by a whole one. A `count` above seven makes a slider too: past seven choices, a slider reads better than a row of radios.

## Showing an average

`isReadOnly` shows any value, fractions included, as one picture: the last star fills to the fraction. It takes no focus and no clicks, and screen readers read its name with the value, "Average rating: 4.3 stars".

## Your own icon

`icon` replaces the star and is drawn twice with `currentColor`: in `color` when filled, muted when empty. Pass `emptyIcon` for a different empty shape, and `getValueLabel` so the spoken names match the picture.

## In a form

With a `name`, the rating is submitted as a number, or as an empty string without one. `isRequired` adds the asterisk, and `aria-required` on a row of star radios, but the browser does not check it, so check the value yourself. Here the error goes back through the [Form](/docs/form/), which shows it under the stars and moves focus to them; the next rating clears it.

## Accessibility

Up to seven stars form a `radiogroup`, one radio per star named "1 star", "2 stars" and so on, with the chosen star, or the first before a rating, as the only tab stop. The arrows stop at the first and last star rather than wrapping round; clearing has keys of its own. Translate the names with `getValueLabel`. The empty stars keep a 3:1 contrast with the page in both themes, and hover previews never change the value.
