## When to use

A Separator draws a line where spacing alone does not part two groups: sign-in options from a form, one day's commits from the next, two sets of buttons in a toolbar. Where a gap already does the job, leave the line out.

## Similar

- [Dropdown](/docs/dropdown/) and [Command](/docs/command/) have separators of their own for their menus; use those inside them.
- [Accordion](/docs/accordion/) draws lines between its items by itself in its `light` and `bordered` variants.

## A word in the middle

Children turn a horizontal separator into two lines with the text between them, in small muted type. The text stays readable by screen readers even while the lines are hidden from them.

## Upright

`orientation="vertical"` stretches to the height of the row. For a shorter line, give it a height and centre it, as between the two groups of buttons here.

## Accessibility

By default the line is decoration: it renders `role="none"` and screen readers pass over it. When it parts groups whose split matters, such as two sets of commands, pass `isDecorative={false}`: it becomes `role="separator"`, and a vertical one also says `aria-orientation="vertical"`.
